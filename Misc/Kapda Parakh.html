Chapter 1 / 12

Quality ka asli matlab

Log bolte hain "accha maal lao, rate reasonable ho". Par good quality ka matlab kisi brand ka naam ya mehnga hona nahi hai. Aapki dukaan ke liye iska simple matlab hai: customer ne kapda kharida, 5 se 6 wash ke baad bhi shape, colour aur silai wese hi hai, aur wo wapas aaya. Yehi aapka repeat customer banata hai, aur chhote town mein repeat customer hi poora business hai.

Pillar 1

Fabric

Kapde ka material (cotton, polyester, blend) aur uska weight, jise GSM bolte hain. Patla ya khurdura fabric jaldi kharab hota hai.

Pillar 2

Stitching

Silai seedhi, tight aur even honi chahiye. Seams ka khulna sabse common complaint hai.

Pillar 3

Finishing

Collar, button, zip, label, size ka naap. Ek hi size ke 3 pieces alag naap ke nahi hone chahiye.

Pillar 4

Wash performance

Dhone ke baad kitna sikudta hai aur rang nikalta hai ya nahi. Ye test dukaan mein nahi, ghar pe hota hai.

Sabse badi galti: sirf haath se chhoo ke ya design dekh ke maal utha lena. Design sabko pasand aata hai, par silai khulne wala piece wapas aake aapki dukaan ki reputation kharab karta hai. Wholesale mein ek defective lot ka matlab hai poora paisa phasa hua.

Chapter 2 / 12

Fabric pehchano

Fabric kharidari ki neev hai. Neeche tab pe click karke har fabric ka haath se feel, fayda, nuksan aur ek chhota test dekho.

Thread burn test (sirf ek loose dhaage pe): cotton kagaz jaisa jalta hai aur halki grey raakh chhodta hai. Polyester pighalta hai aur sakht kaala dana ban jaata hai. Ye test sirf ek chhote dhaage pe karo, kapde pe nahi, aur dukaandaar ki ijazat se. Bahut jagah ye nahi karna padega, haath ka feel aur label kaafi hota hai.

GSM kya hota hai?

GSM matlab Grams per Square Metre, yani 1 square metre kapde ka wazan. Zyada GSM = kapda bhaari aur mota. Par zyada GSM hamesha accha nahi hota. Garmi mein bahut mota t-shirt chubhta hai. Zaroori ye hai ki poore kapde mein GSM ek jaisa ho aur supplier jo bole wohi mile.

0100200300400 GSM

Jersey t-shirt, chiffon aur denim ki ranges standard sourcing guides se hain. Hoodie/fleece ki range thumb rule hai. T-shirt ke liye 180 se 200 GSM comfort aur durability ka accha balance maana jaata hai.

Tool 1GSM khud naapo

Wholesaler bolta hai "200 GSM hai". Sach hai ya nahi? Ek jewellery wali chhoti digital scale (0.01 gram wali, kaafi sasti aati hai) aur ek 10 cm x 10 cm ka card ka tukda kaafi hai. Kapde ko us card ke naap se kaato (ek hi piece ke kinare ya tag ke paas se, jahan wholesaler maane), tukde ka wazan gram mein lo.

Chapter 3 / 12

Stitching: 2 minute ka inspection

Fabric achha ho par silai kharab ho toh kapda bekaar. Har naye supplier ke 3 se 4 random pieces nikalwao (sirf upar rakhe hue nahi, beech ke packet se bhi) aur ye routine chalao:

1

Andar se dekho. Kapde ko ulta karo. Andar ki silai bahar jitni saaf honi chahiye. Latakte dhaage aur uneven overlock matlab jaldbaazi ka kaam.

2

Pull test. Seam ke dono taraf ke kapde ko halka kheencho. Achhi silai na khulti hai na dhaaga dikhta hai. Shoulder, cuff aur hem pe khaas dhyan do.

3

Stitch gino. 1 inch mein kitne tanke hain? Thumb rule: zyada tanke aam taur pe zyada strong silai. Do pieces ko compare karo, jo piece dheela-dheela hai usse bachho.

4

Skipped stitch aur broken thread. Seam ke saath ungli ghumao. Kahin tanka chhoota ho toh wo piece B-grade hai.

5

Collar aur placket. Collar dono taraf barabar aur seedha ho. Shirt ko hanger pe latkao, kapda ek taraf khinch raha ho toh cutting kharab hai.

6

Button aur zip. Button ko khinch ke dekho, thread tight ho. Button-hole ke kinare fray na ho. Zip 5 baar upar neeche karo, atke nahi.

7

Check wale kapde mein pattern match. Pocket aur seam pe lines milni chahiye. Na mile toh sasta kaam hai.

8

Size consistency. Ek hi size ke 3 pieces ka chest aur length naapo. 1 se 2 cm se zyada farak ho toh size label pe bharosa mat karo.

Light test: kapde ko roshni ke saamne pakdo. Jahan patla patch ya chhote ched dikhein, wahan kapda jaldi phatega. Ek hi piece mein kahin mota kahin patla ho toh GSM uneven hai.

Chapter 4 / 12

Wash test: dukaan ke bahar ka sach

Bade orders se pehle ek sample piece kharido (koi bhi wholesaler 1 piece sample de deta hai, ya paise le ke) aur ghar pe wash karo. Wholesale buyers ke liye colour bleed aur shrinkage do sabse badi cheezein hain.

Colour bleed

Safed kapde ke saath dhoo

Dark ya gehre rang ke piece ko ek safed kapde ke saath thande paani mein bhigo do aur 30 minute baad dekho. Safed kapde pe rang aaya toh us piece ko reject karo. Dark colour mein ye non-negotiable hai, kyunki customer ke baaki kapde bhi kharab honge.

Shrinkage

Naap ke dhoo

Dhone se pehle kapde pe 2 nishaan lagao jo ek seedhi line mein ho (jaise 50 cm door). Dhone aur sukhane ke baad dobara naapo. Shrinkage % neeche ke tool se nikaalo.

Shape

Twist aur pilling

Dhone ke baad seam tedhi ho gayi, side seams ghoom gaye ya sataha pe chhote gole (pilling) aa gaye toh kapda kamzor hai.

Tool 2Shrinkage calculator

Shrinkage4%

Thumb rule: 3% tak accha, 3 se 5% chalega par cotton ke liye size ek upar rakhne ka sochna padega, 5% se zyada seedha reject.

Rate ka andaaza

"Reasonable rate" ek number nahi, ek range hoti hai, aur wo kapde ki quality ke saath badalti hai. Neeche online wholesale listings (Indiamart, Surat catalog sites, Tiruppur-Mumbai-Delhi sellers) ka andaaza hai. Ye shuruaati compass hai, final rate nahi. Guwahati ke Fancy Bazar ya aapke apne supplier ka rate alag ho sakta hai.

"60% OFF" ke jhaanse mein mat aao. Online listings mein aksar ₹500 ka MRP likh ke "-60% off" dikhate hain aur asli rate ₹200 hota hai. Ye sirf dikhawa hai. Aap sirf actual per-piece rate aur minimum order quantity (MOQ) dekho.

Apna rate sense kaise banayein (1 hafte ka kaam)

  • Price diary banao. Ek notebook mein har dukaan ka rate likho: item, GSM/quality ka note, rate, MOQ, payment terms.
  • Sirf same quality compare karo. ₹220 ka patla t-shirt aur ₹260 ka 190 GSM t-shirt same cheez nahi hai. GSM tool se naap lo.
  • Kam se kam 3 supplier ke rate lo, tab hi apna "market rate" samajh aayega.
  • Per-piece landed cost nikaalo: rate + transport + packing, kyunki "sasta" wholesaler door ho toh bhada khaa jaata hai.

Chapter 8 / 12

Tag price kaise tay karein

Goreswar mein customer bargain karega, ₹500 ki cheez ₹450 maangega, phir ₹400. Agar aapne tag price bina socche rakha toh bargain mein aapka munafa gayab ho jayega. Isliye pehle bargain ki jagah banao, phir tag lagao.

Markup aur margin alag cheezein hain

Markup
Markup % = profit ÷ cost x 100

Maal ₹500 mein aaya, aapne 40% markup lagaya, toh bechne ka rate ₹700 aur profit ₹200.

Margin
Margin % = profit ÷ selling price x 100

Wahi ₹200 profit, ₹700 ki sale pe sirf 28.6% margin hai, 40% nahi. Asli 40% margin ke liye ₹500 ka maal ₹833 mein bechna padega, yani 66.7% markup.

Yaad rakho: dukaandar jab "40% munafa" bolta hai toh wo aksar markup bolta hai. Asli kamaayi margin se napi jaati hai, aur usme se abhi rent, bijli, helper ki salary aur kharab maal nikalna baaki hai. Chhoti dukaano ke liye 15 se 30% gross margin common bataya jaata hai, bade value-fashion chains lagbhag 33% pe chalte hain.

Chapter 9 / 12

Bargaining: wholesaler se

Ab aap bhi bargain karne wale ho. Wholesaler ke paas bhi room hota hai, aur aapko seekhna hai kaise poochna hai. Wholesale ki bargaining dukaan mein kapde ki bargaining se alag hai: yahan volume, repeat business aur payment aapke hathiyaar hain.

1

Apni pasand mat dikhao. "Wah, kya piece hai!" bolne ke baad rate nahi girta. Shaant raho aur sirf rate poochho.

2

Total bill pe bargain karo. Har piece pe ₹5 kam karwane se bada fayda, poore order pe ek discount maangne mein hai.

3

Competitor ka rate use karo. "Wahan same quality ₹350 mein dikha" bolna sabse powerful line hai, par tabhi bolo jab sach ho. Price diary kaam aayegi.

4

Pehla quote 8 se 15% neeche laane ki koshish karo. Ye thumb rule hai, har item pe alag hoga. Ek-do round mein rate girta hai.

5

Cash ya turant payment ka fayda lo. Cash dene pe discount maango. Shuruat mein udhaar mat lo, kyunki udhaar ka dabaav aapko galat rate pe maal lene pe majboor karta hai.

6

Terms likhwao. Defective piece ka replacement ya return, pakka bill, delivery ki date, size-wise breakup. Bina bill ke maal mat lo.

7

Walk away karna seekho. "Main ek round aur lagake aata hoon" bol ke nikal jao. Aksar aap darwaze tak pahunchte ho aur rate gir jaata hai.

8

Rishta banao. Samay pe paisa do, repeat order do. 3 se 4 orders ke baad wholesaler khud aapko behtar rate aur naye designs pehle dikhata hai.

Pehli kharidari ka plan

Nayi dukaan ka sabse bada khatra ye hai ki aap saara paisa ek hi baar mein stock mein laga do. Ek example split dekho, ₹1,00,000 ke stock budget ke liye (apne budget aur customer ke hisaab se badlo):

55%

₹55,000: Basics

T-shirts, shirts, jeans, innerwear. Ye roz bikne wale items hain.

20%

₹20,000: Seasonal

Hoodies/jackets (sardi), festive wear (Bihu, Durga Puja, Diwali). Season se 3 se 4 hafte pehle laao.

10%

₹10,000: Trial

Naye designs ya trendy items ke 5 se 10 piece. Bika toh repeat, nahi toh chhota nuksan.

15%

₹15,000: Reserve

Jo bika usko dobara bharne ke liye. Ye paisa kabhi khatam mat karo.

  • Size mix: ek pura size-set lene ki jagah apne customers ke hisaab se lo. Men's wear mein M, L, XL aksar sabse zyada bikte hain, S aur XXL kam. Pehle 2 hafte ki sale dekh ke agla order badlo.
  • Chhote lots se shuru karo. Har naye supplier se pehle 1 chhota order (10 se 20 pieces), phir quality ke hisaab se bada.
  • Competition ko dekho. M Baazar jaisi dukaan aur chhote sellers kya kya rakhte hain, kis rate pe bechte hain, ye apni price diary mein likho. Jo wo nahi rakhte (jaise achhi quality ke basics, ya khaas sizes) wahan aapka mauka hai.
  • Hisaab roz likho. Kitna aaya, kitna bika, kya bacha. Ye aadat 6 mahine mein aapko bata degi ki kaunsa maal paisa banata hai.

Chapter 12 / 12

Dukaan ka hisaab: break-even

Aap maal accha kharid lo aur tag price sahi laga do, phir bhi dukaan nuksan mein ho sakti hai agar mahine ka kharcha nikalne jitni sale na ho. Is chapter mein sabse zaroori sawaal ka jawab hai: mahine mein kitni sale chahiye ki nuksan na ho? Ye number break-even point kehlata hai.

Break-even sale (₹/mahina) = mahine ka fixed kharcha ÷ margin %

Fixed kharcha wo hai jo sale ho ya na ho, har mahine dena padta hai:

  • Dukaan ka rent
  • Helper ya staff ki salary
  • Bijli, internet, packing bags aur chhote kharche
  • Loan ki EMI, agar hai
  • Aapki khud ki minimum kamaayi (ghar chalane ke liye jitna chahiye)

Aapki apni salary bhi kharcha hai. Bahut log owner ki kamaayi jodte hi nahi aur sochte hain "dukaan chal rahi hai". Neeche ke tool mein dono numbers alag dikhte hain: bina apni salary ke no-loss point, aur apni salary ke saath asli target.

Margin wahi daalo jo bargain aur kharab maal ke baad bachta hai, tag price ka margin nahi. Shuruat mein 25% jaisi sachchi sankhya rakhna behtar hai. Sale GST ko chhod ke maani gayi hai.

Isse kya samajhna hai: agar aapko dikhe ki roz 25 se 30 walk-in chahiye aur aapke area mein itne log aate hi nahi, toh ya kharcha kam karo (chhoti jagah, kam staff) ya average bill badhao (set bechna, ladies aur kids bhi rakhna). Dukaan kholne se pehle ye number pata hona chahiye, baad mein nahi.

GST ke basics

  • Registration kab: goods bechne wale ko saalana turnover ek limit se upar jaane pe GST registration zaroori hota hai. Assam ke liye goods ki limit ₹40 lakh batayi gayi hai, par apne CA ya GST suvidha kendra se current limit confirm karo.
  • Rate: readymade garments par 5% GST hai jab tak ek piece ki sale value ₹2,500 tak ho, aur uske upar 18% (22 September 2025 se). Ye sale value pe lagta hai, MRP pe nahi.
  • Supplier se poochho: rate GST ke saath hai ya bina? Agar aap registered nahi ho toh supplier ko diya GST aapki cost ka hissa ban jaata hai. Isse landed cost mein jodna mat bhoolo.
  • Hamesha GST bill lo. Pakka bill aapko kharab maal ya vivaad mein bhi bachata hai.

Udhaar

Customer ka khata

Sirf jaane-pehchaane customer ko, ek limit tak, aur tareekh likh ke. Register mein naam, rakam aur wada ki tareekh. Shuruat mein wholesaler se udhaar mat lo, aur customer ko bhi kam do.

Shuruaati kharch ka andaaza: ek source ke mutabik chhote town mein cloth shop kholne ka kharch ₹50,000 se ₹5 lakh ke beech aa sakta hai. Ye bahut bada farak hai, isliye pehle apne rent, furniture aur stock ka alag-alag hisaab likho, phir hi paisa lagao.

Yaad rakhne ke liye

12 line ka cheat sheet

  1. Quality = fabric + stitching + finishing + wash. Chaaron dekho.
  2. T-shirt ke liye 180 se 200 GSM sweet spot hai. Scale se khud naapo.
  3. Andar se silai dekho, pull test karo, roshni mein kapda dekho.
  4. Bade order se pehle 1 sample dhoke dekho: rang aur shrinkage.
  5. 3 "MUST" checks fail hue toh maal mat lo.
  6. Price diary mein kam se kam 3 supplier ke same-quality rate likho.
  7. Landed cost = rate + transport + packing. Wahi aapka asli cost hai.
  8. Tag price = pehle bargain ki jagah, phir margin. Markup aur margin mat milao.
  9. Pehla quote 8 se 15% neeche laao, aur walk away se mat daro.
  10. Budget ka 15% reserve rakho, aur bina bill ke maal mat lo.
  11. Kurti ke sets mein kurta, pant aur dupatta ek hi shade ke ho. Kids ke kapdon mein button aur dori ki safety dekho.
  12. Mahine ka no-loss point nikaalo: kharcha ÷ margin %. Isse kam sale pe dukaan nuksan mein chalti hai.
