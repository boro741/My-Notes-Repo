01

Why maths decides if the shop survives

Ek shop customers se bhari ho sakti hai aur phir bhi loss mein chal sakti hai. Aisa tab hota hai jab selling price aapki kharid se bas thoda upar ho, jab aap bina profit check kiye discount de dete ho, ya jab rent, bijli aur helper ki salary jaise costs bhool jaate ho.

Ulta bhi sach hai: shop shaant ho tab bhi achha kama sakti hai, agar har sale mein kaafi profit ho. Farak sirf arithmetic ka hai, aur arithmetic mushkil nahi. Bas jod, ghata, guna, bhag aur ek idea: percentage.

Why this mattersAap hazaaron chhote decisions loge: kya kharidna hai, kitna price lena hai, bargain par kitna neeche jaana hai. Agar feeling se decide karoge, toh kabhi-kabhi cost se neeche bech doge aur pata bhi nahi chalega. Numbers se decide karoge, toh har sale ka profit pata hoga.

Is guide mein paanch calculators hain. Padhte waqt unhe use karo. End tak aap zyaadatar hisaab dimaag mein hi kar loge.

Naye owners ki 6 common galtiyan
  1. Wholesaler ke bill price se hi tag lagana, transport aur packing bhool jaana. (Chapter 5)
  2. "Cost ka double" rakh dena bina dekhe ki market mein wahi shirt kitne mein mil rahi hai. (Chapter 6)
  3. Bargain mein floor price pata hi nahi hona. (Chapters 6, 7)
  4. Margin aur markup ko ek samajhna. (Chapter 4)
  5. Pehli baar mein bahut zyada stock le lena. (Chapters 10, 13)
  6. Shop ka paisa aur ghar ka paisa mila dena, aur records na rakhna. (Chapters 13, 14)

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Meri maths kamzor hai. Kya main yeh sab kar paunga?

Haan. Sirf chaar kaam aate hain: jod, ghata, guna, bhag. Mobile ka calculator aur is guide ke calculators aapka kaam kar denge. Shuru ke ek mahine mein har baar calculate karo. Uske baad aapko 5 ya 6 numbers yaad ho jaayenge (jaise har item type ka floor price), aur baaki kaam dimaag mein ho jayega.

Kya mujhe har customer ke saamne calculation karni padegi?

Nahi. Calculation pehle karni hai, customer aane se pehle. Har item type ka floor price (sabse neeche ka price) ek baar nikaal ke likh lo. Phir counter par sirf yeh dekhna hai: "customer ka offer floor se upar hai ya neeche?". Chapter 7 mein yahi sikhenge.

Gross profit vs net profit

Gross profit = selling price − landed cost. Yeh sirf kapde ka profit hai, shop ke kharche se pehle.

Net profit = poore mahine ka gross profit − rent, salary, bijli, loan interest aur baaki fixed costs (aur tax). Yahi paisa asal mein aapke paas bachta hai.

Is guide mein chapter 4 se 8 tak "profit" ka matlab gross profit hai. Chapter 9 mein hum dekhenge ki gross profit se rent kaise nikalta hai.

Why this mattersPricing mein zyaadatar confusion in words ko mix karne se hota hai, khaaskar margin aur markup. Chapter 4 mein yeh saaf ho jayega.
03

Percentages from zero

Percent ka matlab hai 100 mein se. Toh 20% ka matlab hua har 100 mein se 20.

Agar kisi cheez ki cost ₹100 hai aur aap 20% lete ho, toh ₹20 liye. Agar cost ₹500 hai, toh usmein paanch sau hain, isliye 5 × ₹20 = ₹100. Percentage bas itna hi hai: ek fraction jiske neeche 100 hai.

The one formula Percent of a number = number × percent ÷ 100
20% of ₹500 = 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹100

Quick tricks to do it in your head

  • 10%: aakhri digit hata do. ₹640 ka 10% hai ₹64.
  • 5%: 10% ka aadha. ₹640 ka 5% hai ₹32.
  • 20%: 10% ko double karo. ₹640 ka 20% hai ₹128.
  • 25%: 4 se bhag do. ₹640 ka 25% hai ₹160.
  • 50%: 2 se bhag do.
  • 15%: 10% plus 5%. ₹640 ka 15% hai ₹64 + ₹32 = ₹96.

Going the other way: what percent is this?

Ek customer ko ₹500 ki shirt par ₹50 off diya. Yeh kitna percent discount hua?

Percent = part ÷ whole × 100
50 ÷ 500 × 100 = 10%

Price ko seedha kam ya zyada karna

Calculator ke bina bhi ek shortcut kaam aata hai. Percent ko 100 mein se ghatao ya 100 mein jodo, phir decimal banake guna karo.

20% off: price × 0.80  →  ₹549 × 0.80 = ₹439
5% GST add: price × 1.05  →  ₹500 × 1.05 = ₹525
The most important habit

Har percentage kisi cheez ka percentage hota hai. Use karne se pehle poochho: percent of what? Us "what" ko base kehte hain.

Discount % ka base hai tag price. Margin % ka base hai selling price. Markup % ka base hai cost. Alag base, toh same rupees ke liye alag number aata hai. Shops mein zyaadatar galtiyan yahi bhool jaane se hoti hain.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Customer bolta hai "20% kam karo". Kisme se 20%?

Customer ka matlab hamesha tag price ka 20% hota hai. Tag ₹549 hai toh 20% = ₹110, aur woh ₹439 dena chahega. Aapko isse apne floor price (₹434) se compare karna hai. Chapter 7 mein poora example hai.

04

Profit: margin and markup

Ek piece ka profit simple hai:

Profit = Selling Price − Landed Cost

Ab ek shirt lo jo aapko landed ₹330 padi aur ₹440 mein bik gayi. Profit hua ₹110. Is ₹110 ko do alag percentages se bata sakte hain.

Same shirt, two percentages
Landed cost₹330
Selling price₹440
Profit₹110
Markup = 110 ÷ 330 × 100 (base: cost)33.3%
Margin = 110 ÷ 440 × 100 (base: selling price)25%

Margin or markup? The difference, made simple

Margin aur markup do alag profit nahi hain. Yeh wahi ₹110 hai, bas percentage mein do alag tareeke se likha gaya. Farak sirf is mein hai ki aap ₹110 ko kis se divide karte ho.

  1. Apni cost nikalo. Yeh landed cost hai: supplier ka price + transport + loading + packing (chapter 5). Hamari shirt ke liye: ₹330.
  2. Rupees mein profit nikalo. Selling price − cost = 440 − 330 = ₹110. Margin aur markup dono ke liye yeh number same hai.
  3. ₹110 ko percentage banao. Yahan dono alag hote hain:
    • Markup: profit ko cost se divide karo. 110 ÷ 330 = 33%
    • Margin: profit ko selling price se divide karo. 110 ÷ 440 = 25%
Margin: profit compared with the whole selling price
Cost ₹330Profit ₹110
Base = poori bar, ₹440. Toh 110 ÷ 440 = 25%.
Markup: profit compared with the cost only
Cost ₹330Profit ₹110
Base = sirf cost wala hissa, ₹330. Toh 110 ÷ 330 = 33%.

Laal hissa wahi hai, par bade base (₹440) se naapo toh percentage chhota aata hai. Isliye markup hamesha bada number hota hai.

Remember it like this

Mark-UP aapki cost se upar dekhta hai: "maine upar kitna rakha?"

Margin customer ke paise ko dekhta hai: "unhone jo diya, usmein se kitna mera hai?"

Rupees same, base alag. Decision margin se lo.

The formulas

Margin % = (SP − Cost) ÷ SP × 100
Markup % = (SP − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Price from a target SP from target margin: SP = Cost ÷ (1 − margin)
SP from target markup: SP = Cost × (1 + markup)

Pehle wale ko check karo: cost ₹330, target margin 25% (yaani 0.25). SP = 330 ÷ 0.75 = ₹440. Sahi.

Where you use each one

Use margin at your counter. Ek customer ₹440 wali shirt ke liye ₹400 offer karta hai. Profit hoga ₹70, aur ₹400 mein se ₹70 hai 17.5%. Agar aap kabhi 20% se neeche nahi jaate, toh aap mana kar doge. Yeh decision seconds mein ho jata hai kyunki margin us paise ka hissa hai jo customer de raha hai.

Know markup when a supplier quotes it. Ek wholesaler bolta hai "main apni cost mein 20% add karta hoon". Uski cost ₹250 hai, toh woh ₹300 mein bechta hai. Yeh 20% markup hai, par uska margin sirf 50 ÷ 300 = 16.7% hai. Agar aap 20% off maangoge, toh woh ₹240 par hoga, jo uski apni cost ₹250 se neeche hai. Farak jaanne se pata chalta hai ki woh bargain mein asal mein kitna neeche aa sakta hai.

This is not your take-home

Yahan margin shop ke kharche se pehle ka hai. Har ₹100 mein se jo ₹25 bache, unhi se rent, bijli, helper, loan EMI aur aapki apni salary deni hai. Chapter 9 mein dekhenge ki iske liye kitni shirts bechni padengi.

Common trap

Koi supplier ya dost bolta hai "30% profit rakho". Shirt aapko ₹330 padi. Poochho: 30% kis cheez ka? Do jawab hain.

  • Cost ka 30% (markup). ₹330 ka 30% hai ₹99. Jod do: 330 + 99 = ₹429.
  • Selling price ka 30% (margin). Aap chahte ho ki customer ke har ₹100 mein se ₹30 aapke ho, toh cost ke liye 70% bachta hai. Divide karo: 330 ÷ 0.70 = ₹471. Check: profit ₹141, aur 141 ÷ 471 = 30%.

Words same, par ek shirt par ₹42 ka farak (471 − 429). 500 shirts bechi toh 42 × 500 = ₹21,000. Isliye hamesha poochho ki unka matlab kaunsa hai.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Mujhe sirf markup se price set karna aasan lagta hai. Kya galat hai?

Galat nahi hai, "cost × 1.33" dimaag mein karna aasan hai. Bas result ko margin mein check karo: 330 × 1.33 = ₹440, aur ₹440 par margin 25% hai. Agar 25% aapke minimum se upar hai toh theek. Pehle markup se price nikalo, phir margin se decision lo.

100% markup ka matlab 100% margin hota hai kya?

Nahi. 100% markup matlab price cost ka double: ₹330 ki shirt ₹660 mein. Profit ₹330, aur ₹330 ÷ ₹660 = 50% margin. Margin kabhi 100% nahi hota jab tak cost zero na ho.

Customer ko kya batana chahiye, margin ya markup?

Kuch nahi. Customer ko sirf price se matlab hai. Margin aur markup aapke apne hisaab ke numbers hain. Customer ke saamne apna margin kabhi mat bolo.

Isme rent aur bijli kahan hai?

Is chapter ke numbers mein nahi hain. Margin sirf yeh batata hai ki kapde se kitna bacha. Rent, bijli, salary alag hain aur chapter 9 mein judte hain. Dono ko mat mix karo.

05

What one shirt really costs you

Wholesaler ke bill ka price aapki cost nahi hai. Maan lo aap kisi wholesale market se (jaise Guwahati ka Fancy Bazar) 40 shirts ₹300 ke hisaab se kharidte ho. Phir unhe ghar tak laane ka kharcha bhi dena padta hai.

Buying 40 shirts
40 shirts × ₹300₹12,000
Transport (bus or truck)₹800
Loading and unloading₹200
Polythene, tags, hangers₹200
Total spent₹13,200
Per shirt (₹13,200 ÷ 40)₹330

Ab ek aur sachchai jodo. Kuch pieces kharab nikalte hain, stitching fault ke saath aate hain, ya hamesha unsold rehte hain. Maan lo 40 mein se 2 kabhi nahi bikte. Aapne ₹13,200 kharch kiye, par sirf 38 pieces hi paisa wapas laayenge.

Landed cost per sellable piece = total spent ÷ pieces you can sell
13,200 ÷ 38 = ₹347

Yeh ₹347 aapki asli cost hai. Har price isi se shuru karo, ₹300 se nahi.

Why this mattersAgar aap ₹300 se price set karoge, toh sochoge ki ₹440 ki shirt par ₹140 kamate ho. ₹347 se shuru karoge toh asal mein ₹93 kamate ho. Pehla number ek achhi kahani hai. Doosra woh number hai jo sach mein har shirt aapko deti hai.

Dhyan do: ₹347 sirf shirt ki cost hai. Rent, bijli, helper ki salary, loan EMI aur aapki apni pay isme nahi hain, kyunki woh har shirt bikne ke saath nahi badalte. Har shirt par ₹93 woh paisa hai jo inhe dene ke liye available hai. Chapter 9 mein hum mahine ke yeh kharche jodte hain aur dekhte hain ki unhe cover karne ke liye kitni shirts bechni padengi. Uske baad jo bachta hai, wahi aapka asli profit hai.

Neeche calculators mein "damaged or unsold allowance" yahi step aapke liye kar deta hai. 3% se 5% se shuru karo aur experience ke saath adjust karo.

Anubhav se

Pehli baar maal aaye toh bill ke saath ek chhoti list bana lo: kapde ka paisa, gaadi, coolie, packing, aur khaane-peene ka kharcha jo sirf maal laane ke liye hua. Sab jodo aur pieces se divide karo. Yeh 10 minute ka kaam hai, aur yahi aapki asli landed cost hai.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Landed cost mein rent aur electricity kyun nahi jodte?

Kyunki rent ₹8,000 hai chahe aap 10 shirts bechein ya 200. Woh ek shirt se nahi jude. Landed cost sirf woh kharche hain jo is maal ko laane mein hue. Rent aur baaki fixed kharche chapter 9 (break-even) mein alag se judte hain.

"Damaged or unsold" kitna maanu? 3%, 5% ya 10%?

Shuru mein 5% rakho, thoda safe side par. Ek-do mahine baad apna asli number dekho: kitne pieces kharab aaye, kitne bilkul nahi bike. Phir allowance badal do. Naye owner aksar isse kam andaza lagate hain, aur baad mein pachtate hain.

Wholesaler se bill lena zaroori hai?

Haan. Bill ke bina aapke paas na cost ka proof hai, na GST ka record, aur kharab maal wapas karne ki baat karna mushkil hota hai. Har kharid ka pucca bill ya parchi lo aur uski photo phone mein rakho.

06

Setting a price when customers bargain

Goreswar mein aapko pata hai ki customer kam price maangega. Isliye jo number aap pehle bolte ho, yaani tag price, itna high hona chahiye ki bargain ke baad bhi, sabse kam price par jo aap accept karte ho, usme profit bacha rahe.

Teen steps mein ulta chalo.

  1. Landed cost. Chapter 5 se. Example: ₹347.
  2. Floor price. Sabse kam price jo aap accept karte ho. Floor par ek minimum margin choose karo jisse aap khush ho, maan lo 20%. Floor = Cost ÷ (1 − 0.20) = 347 ÷ 0.80 = ₹434.
  3. Tag price. Sabse bada bargain decide karo jo aap allow karoge, maan lo tag ka 20% off. Tag = Floor ÷ (1 − 0.20) = 434 ÷ 0.80 = ₹543. Isse ek friendly price tak round up karo, jaise ₹549.
Floor = Cost ÷ (1 − minimum margin)
Tag = Floor ÷ (1 − biggest bargain)
Together Tag = Cost ÷ [(1 − minimum margin) × (1 − biggest bargain)]
Floor price for this shirt₹434Landed ₹347, minimum margin 20%. Tag ₹549.

₹549 ke tag par 20% bargain se price ₹439 ho jata hai. Yeh aapke ₹434 floor se upar hai, toh aapko phir bhi lagbhag 21% margin milta hai. Agar customer poora ₹549 de de toh margin lagbhag 37% hai. Har customer ko bargain karne ki zaroorat nahi. Jo poora price dete hain, woh un logon ki kami poori kar dete hain jo zyada dabate hain.

Anubhav se

Shuru mein har ek item ke liye alag calculation mat karo. Items ko 3 ya 4 groups mein baanto (jaise basic T-shirts, formal shirts, jeans, kurtis), har group ka ek typical landed cost lo, aur us group ka floor aur tag nikaal lo. Baad mein jab sales ka pattern samajh aaye tab item-wise karo.

Round figure (₹549, ₹499) isliye achha lagta hai ki customer ko ₹550 ya ₹500 se thoda kam lagta hai. Zyaadatar dukaandar yahi karte hain.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Main cost ka seedha double kyun nahi rakh sakta?

Double matlab 100% markup, yaani 50% margin. ₹330 ki shirt ₹660 mein. Agar nearby shops mein wahi shirt ₹500 mein mil rahi hai toh aapki ₹660 wali shirt koi nahi lega. Price aapki cost se nahi, market ke ceiling se bhi bandha hota hai. Chapter 9 mein "maximum margin" yahi batata hai.

Customer bolta hai "dusri shop mein ₹499 mein mil rahi hai". Ab?

Apna floor dekho. Is shirt ka floor ₹434 hai, toh ₹499 par aap ₹152 kamate ho aur margin 30.5% rehta hai. Aap match kar sakte ho. Agar unka price aapke floor se neeche hota (jaise ₹400) toh match mat karo. Shayad woh loss mein bech rahe hain ya unki cost kam hai. Aap size, quality aur service se jeeto, loss se nahi.

Kya main ek hi tag price sab par laga doon, jaise sab T-shirt ₹499?

Sirf tab jab sabki landed cost lagbhag same ho. Agar ek T-shirt ₹250 ki hai aur doosri ₹350 ki, toh ₹499 par pehli par bahut margin aur doosri par bahut kam margin hoga. Group ke andar cost jitni kareeb ho, ek price utna theek chalta hai.

Rules for bargaining without losing money

  • Roz shop kholne se pehle apna floor jaano. Tag ke peeche ek code mein likh do jo sirf aap aur aapka helper samjhe. Phir customer ke saamne kisi ko calculate nahi karna padta.
  • Chhote steps mein neeche aao. ₹549 se ₹520, phir ₹499, phir ₹470. Chhote steps se customer ko dikhta hai ki aap sach mein hil rahe ho, aur floor tak pahunchne mein time lagta hai.
  • Har kami ke saath ek wajah do. "Aap do le rahe ho" ya "pehli baar aa rahe ho". Customer ko lagta hai unhone yeh kamaya hai.
  • Price nahi, pieces zyada becho. "Do shirts ₹950 mein" se har piece ka price thoda kam hota hai, par ek customer se profit ke rupees double ho jaate hain. Pehle har piece par floor check karo.
  • Mana karna bilkul theek hai. Floor se neeche muskurao aur jaane do. Doosra customer sahi price dega, aur jo gaya woh aksar wapas aata hai.
  • Helper ko sirf floor ke andar bargain karne do. Har item type ka written floor do, aur floor se upar bechne par chhota sa inaam.
Why this mattersAapki bargaining limit ek business rule hai, feeling nahi. Jab rule pehle se tay ho, toh kisi bhi sale ke liye neend kharab nahi hoti.
Anubhav se
  • Pehla offer kabhi seedha accept mat karo, chahe woh floor se upar ho. Ek chhota counter zaroor do. Customer ko lagta hai unhone bargain jeeta, aur aap ₹20 se ₹30 zyada kamate ho.
  • Jo customer bina bargain ke poora price de, use khush rakho. Wahi aapke margin ko upar le jata hai.
  • Roz shaam ko 5 minute lo aur dekho: aaj sabse zyada kis price par deals band hui? Yahi aapke asli "market price" ka sabse accha signal hai.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Customer ek saath 3 shirts le raha hai aur bulk discount maangta hai. Kitna dun?

Pehle total profit dekho. Agar har shirt par ₹100 profit hai, toh teen par ₹300. Total ₹60 (₹20 per shirt) ki kami dena theek hai, kyunki profit abhi ₹240 hai aur aap teen pieces nikaal rahe ho. Bas har piece par floor ke upar raho.

Customer aaya, bahut dekha, par bina kharide chala gaya. Kya main kuch galat kar raha hoon?

Zaroori nahi. Zyaadatar customers pehli baar mein nahi kharidte. Dekho kya wapas aate hain, aur unse pooch lo ki unhe kya chahiye tha (size, colour, price). Ek notebook mein "maanga par nahi tha" likh lo. Yeh aapki next buying ki list ban jayegi.

08

Discounts and offers: the trap

Discount chhote dikhte hain par bahut mehnge padte hain. Ek shirt lo jiski landed cost ₹70 hai aur ₹100 mein bikti hai. Margin 30% hai, toh aap har shirt par ₹30 kamate ho. Ab 10% off do: price ₹90 ho gaya aur cost abhi bhi ₹70, toh profit gir ke ₹20 reh gaya.

10% discount ne aapka profit ek-tihai kam kar diya, kyunki profit cost ke baad bacha hua chhota sa hissa hai, aur discount usi chhote hisse mein se nikalta hai.

If margin is m and discount is d (both as decimals) New margin = (m − d) ÷ (1 − d)
Sales needed to earn the same total profit = m ÷ (m − d) × your old sales

Yahan m = 0.30 aur d = 0.10. New margin = 0.20 ÷ 0.90 = 22%. Sales needed = 0.30 ÷ 0.20 = 1.5 guna. Pehle jitna kamate the utna hi kamane ke liye aapko 50% zyada shirts bechni padengi.

Two discounts in a row do not add up

"10% off, aur bill par 5% aur off" matlab 15% off nahi hota. Doosra discount pehle se kam hue price par lagta hai. ₹1,000 par: 10% off se ₹900, phir ₹900 ka 5% hai ₹45, toh customer dega ₹855. Yeh kul 14.5% off hua. 15% se thoda kam, jo aapke liye achha hai, par asli number jaano.

Safer ways to give value
  • Bundle offer do: "2 for ₹950". Customer ko deal lagti hai aur aap do pieces nikaalte ho.
  • Kuch sasta add kar do jo aapko kam padta hai: free alteration, ya ₹15 wale ek jode moze.
  • Clearance sirf purane stock par karo, naye arrivals par nahi (chapter 10).
  • "Flat 50% off" tab tak advertise mat karo jab tak check na kar lo ki aapka tag price isse allow karta hai. Pehle calculator 1 use karo.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Festival par "Flat 30% off" laga sakta hoon kya?

Is shirt ke liye (landed ₹347, tag ₹549): 549 × 0.70 = ₹384. Profit sirf ₹37, aur margin lagbhag 10%. Yeh aapke 20% minimum se aadha hai, aur rent nikalne ke liye bahut kam. Agar festival offer chahiye toh "10% off" ya "2 for ₹950" jaise offers chuno, ya sirf slow items par discount do.

Sale se customers aayenge, toh volume se profit nahi badhega?

Kabhi-kabhi, par calculator 3 se check karo. 30% margin par 10% discount ke liye 50% zyada sales chahiye. Kya aapke paas utne customers aur utna stock hai? Agar nahi, toh discount se profit girega, badhega nahi.

09

Break-even and how much margin you need

Ek shirt ka profit aapka profit nahi hai. Shirts se jo paisa bana, usme se shop ke mahine ke fixed kharche dene padte hain. Jo bachta hai, wahi aapka hai.

Break-even woh sales hai jahan aapne har fixed cost chuka diya aur kamaya bilkul zero. Isse neeche loss, isse upar kamai.

Rent kahan se aata hai? Step by step

Rent, salary aur bijli tab bhi dene padte hain jab ek bhi customer shop mein na aaye. Yeh paisa kapde ki sale ke gross profit se aata hai. Margin batata hai ki har sale mein se kitna us "rent pool" mein jata hai.

  1. Har ₹100 ki sale mein se kitna aapka hai?Margin ka matlab yahi hai. 28% margin par ₹100 ki sale mein se ₹72 supplier ke liye gaye aur ₹28 aapke paas bache.
  2. Rent unhi ₹28 se nikalta hai.Rent, salary aur bijli supplier wale ₹72 se nahi nikalte. Sirf bache hue ₹28 se nikalte hain.
  3. Ab ginti karo: kitni sale chahiye?Maan lo fixed kharche ₹30,000 hain. Har ₹100 se ₹28 bachte hain, toh 30,000 ÷ 28 = 1,071 baar ₹100, yaani lagbhag ₹1,07,143 ki sales.

Example: costs ₹30,000, average margin 28% → 30,000 ÷ 0.28 = ₹1,07,143

How much margin do you need? Three numbers

Koi ek "sahi" margin nahi hota. Aapko teen numbers chahiye: neeche ki line (survival margin), jo aap chahte ho (target margin), aur upar ki chhat jo market lagata hai (ceiling margin). Pehle do aapke apne kharche se nikalte hain. Teesra market se.

  1. Survival margin = fixed costs ÷ expected monthly sales. Isse kam margin par har mahine loss. Example: ₹30,000 ÷ ₹1,50,000 = 20%.
  2. Target margin = (fixed costs + profit you want) ÷ expected monthly sales. Example: (30,000 + 15,000) ÷ 1,50,000 = 30%.
  3. Ceiling margin = (market price − your cost) ÷ market price. Nearby shops mein similar shirt ₹549 mein bik rahi hai, aapki cost ₹347 hai: (549 − 347) ÷ 549 = 36.8%.

Teeno ko saath dekho. Agar ceiling target se upar hai, toh item achha hai. Agar ceiling target se neeche par survival se upar hai, toh item kharche nikalta hai par aapka profit goal nahi. Agar ceiling survival se bhi neeche hai, toh woh item apni cost nahi nikaal sakta. Cost negotiate karo, ya chhod do, ya jaan-bujhkar sirf footfall ke liye rakho.

Yaad rakho: chapter 6 mein aapne floor par jo minimum margin rakha, woh kabhi bhi survival margin se neeche nahi hona chahiye. Isi liye us example mein floor margin 20% hai.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Mujhe pata hi nahi ki mera pehla mahine kitna bikega. Break-even kaise nikaalun?

Bilkul normal hai. Pehle mahine ke liye estimate mat banao, sirf fixed costs jodo aur apna expected margin (28% jaisa) rakho, aur "kitna bechna hai" ka target nikaal lo. Pehle mahine ke baad asli sales aur asli margin daal ke calculator dobara chalao. Do-teen mahine mein aapka sahi number mil jayega.

Break-even se upar jo bacha woh poora mera hai?

Lagbhag, par poora nahi. Usme se tax, loan ki principal wapas karna, naya stock kharidna (shop ko chalte rehne ke liye) aur emergency cushion nikalna padega. Jo sach mein ghar le ja sakte ho woh isse kam hota hai. Isliye apni fixed salary pehle se costs mein jodo, aur baaki ko shop mein rehne do.

Mera average margin 28% hai, par bargaining ke baad asal mein kitna hota hai?

Kam hota hai. Tag par 37% margin hai, par agar customer average 10% kam paise dete hain toh asli margin lagbhag 30% ho jata hai (tag ₹549, price ₹494: (494 − 347) ÷ 494). Isliye break-even ke liye hamesha "after bargaining" wala margin lo.

10

Stock, cash and dead stock

Stock woh paisa hai jo aapne kapde mein badal diya. Jab tak woh bik nahi jata, aap us paise ko rent, salary ya naya maal kharidne ke liye use nahi kar sakte. ₹2 lakh ka stock aur ₹500 drawer mein wali shop musibat mein hai, chahe woh "worth" kitni bhi ho.

How fast stock sells

Gino is mahine kitne pieces bike, aur mahine ke end mein kitne pieces haath mein hain, phir divide karo.

Months to sell the stock = pieces in stock ÷ pieces sold per month
300 pieces in stock, 100 sold per month → 3 months

Agar koi item type 6 mahine ya zyada mein bikta hai, toh agli baar kam kharido. Agar doosra do hafte mein sold out ho jata hai, toh zyada aur pehle kharido.

A markdown plan for slow pieces

Pehle se decide kar lo ki ek piece kitne din rakhne ke baad uska price girega. Example (apne numbers ke hisaab se badlo):

  • Mahina 1 se 2: poora tag price, normal bargaining ke saath.
  • Mahina 3: tag lagbhag 15% se 20% kam karo.
  • Mahina 4: landed cost ke kareeb par clear karo aur paisa aise item mein lagao jo bikta hai.

Purani shirt ko cost par bech dena usse hamesha ke liye rakhne se behtar hai, kyunki us paise se nayi shirt kharid sakte ho jo profit degi.

Buy for your customers and the calendar

Assam mein demand festivals aur mausam ke saath badalti hai: Bohag Bihu, Magh Bihu, Durga Puja, Eid, Diwali, Christmas, shaadi ke mahine, school reopening aur sardi. Har ek ke liye kuch hafte pehle kharidna shuru karo. Sizes bhi plan karo: sirf medium aur large rakhne wali shop un customers ko kho degi jinhe small aur extra large chahiye.

Keep a cash reserve

Ek common safety rule hai ki stock ke bahar cash mein kai mahine ke fixed costs rakho, taaki slow mahine mein cost se neeche bechna na pade. Kam se kam do se teen mahine ka lakshya rakho.

Be careful with credit (udhar)

Agar customer baad mein paisa deta hai, toh aapka profit kaagaz par hai par cash phasa hua hai. Udhar sirf unhe do jinhe achhe se jaante ho, limit tay karo, aur har amount date ke saath notebook mein likho. Kam margin wale items par udhar mat do.

Anubhav se
  • Shuruaat mein stock zyada nahi, variety zyada. Har design ke 10 pieces ki jagah 4 ya 5 designs ke 2 ya 3 pieces lo. Dekho kya bikta hai, phir sirf winners dobara mangao.
  • Size-wise count rakho. Jab sirf XL aur XXL bache hon toh item slow ho jata hai. Dobara order karte waqt missing sizes pehle bharo.
  • Hafte mein ek baar stock gino. Bills ke hisaab se jitna hona chahiye, shelf par utna hai ya nahi. Gap ka matlab chori, galat billing ya bina bill ke maal gaya.
  • Chhote kharche bhi count karo: packing bags, alteration, repairs, UPI charges. Yeh chhote lagte hain par mahine ke end mein margin kha jaate hain.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Pehle order mein kitna stock lun?

Rule of thumb: shuru mein itna stock lo jo lagbhag 4 se 6 hafte ki expected sales ke barabar ho (cost ke hisaab se), aur uske baad sirf winners dobara mangao. Agar aapko pata nahi ki kitna bikega, toh kam lo. Kam stock se thoda chook sakte ho, par zyada stock se paisa phasta hai, aur woh zyada mehenga padta hai. Sizes ka mix wholesaler se poochho, aur apne customers ke hisaab se adjust karo. Aam taur par beech ke sizes zyada bikte hain, par apne town ka pattern dekho.

Sirf kuch sizes bache hain, baaki bik gaye. Kya karun?

Un bachi hui sizes ko ek alag rack par "last pieces" ke naam se rakho, thoda kam price par. Sabse pehle apna markdown rule dekho. Agli baar wholesaler se woh sizes pehle mangao jo bik gaye.

Helper bill kiye bina kapde de de ya paisa rakh le toh?

Isse rokne ke simple tareeke: har sale ka bill (ya billing app mein entry), hafte mein stock count, aur shuru ke mahino mein cash drawer aap khud sambhalo. Staff ko saaf batao ki discount kitna de sakte hain (floor) aur kab aapse poochna hai. Bharose ke saath checking dono rakho.

Return ya exchange ki policy kya rakhun?

Pehle se likh lo aur counter par laga do: jaise "exchange 7 din mein, bill ke saath, tag laga ho, dhula na ho". Cash refund sirf kharabi par. Customer aur staff dono ko clarity milti hai, aur jhagde kam hote hain. Apne area ke rules ke liye CA ya local dukaandar se poochh lo.

11

GST and paperwork, briefly

GST ek tax hai jo customer ke diye hue paise mein judta hai. Aap use collect karte ho aur government ko dete ho. Yeh na aapka profit hai, na aapki cost, isliye ise apne margin ke hisaab mein kabhi mat milao.

22 September 2025 se lage rate changes ke hisaab se, readymade garments par ₹2,500 per piece tak 5% aur usse upar 18% GST lagne ki report hai (CAclubindia report). Rates aur thresholds badalte rehte hain, isliye pehla bill print karne se pehle current rule confirm kar lo.

If your tag price already includes 5% GST Price before tax = price with GST ÷ 1.05
₹549 ÷ 1.05 = ₹523 before tax, ₹26 is GST.

Abhi decide karo ki aapke tag prices mein GST included hoga ya nahi. Aapka margin GST se pehle wale price par nikalna chahiye, kyunki tax government ko jata hai. Registration aapki saalana sales aur aapke state par depend karta hai. Pehle hafte mein kisi local chartered accountant (CA) se mil lo. Ek chhoti visit sasti hai aur woh galtiyan bachati hai jo mehengi padti hain.

CA se pehle hafte mein yeh poochho
  1. Mere liye GST registration abhi zaroori hai ya nahi? Kab zaroori hoga?
  2. Kya mere liye koi simple scheme (jaise composition) sahi rahegi, ya regular GST?
  3. Shop ke liye kaun si licence ya registration chahiye (shop and establishment, trade licence, Udyam)?
  4. Garments par bill ka sahi format kya hai, aur MRP ya tag par kya likhna zaroori hai?
  5. Shop ka alag current account kab aur kaise kholun?
  6. Mujhe saal mein kaun se returns file karne honge, aur unki kya dates hain?

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Kya bina GST registration ke shop shuru kar sakta hoon?

Yeh aapki expected sales aur state ke rules par depend karta hai. Yahan main ek pakka jawab nahi de sakta, isliye yeh sawaal CA se pehle hafte mein hi poochho. Jawab chahe jo bhi ho, apne bill aur records pehle din se saaf rakhna aage kaam aayega.

Bill dena zaroori hai kya, ya sirf customer maange tab?

Rules aapki registration ki sthiti par alag hote hain. CA se confirm karo. Business ke liye faida yeh hai ki har sale ka record banta hai, stock apne aap kam hota hai (billing app mein), aur kharab maal wapas aane par proof rehta hai. Chapter 14 mein billing ke options hain.

12

Competing with M Baazar and the tiny shops

Aapke saamne ek bada shop hai aur aapke bagal mein chhote shops. Maths aapko batata hai ki kahan compete karna hai. Yeh suggestions hain test karne ke liye, guarantee nahi.

Do a price survey in week one

M Baazar aur do chhote shops mein jao. 20 popular items (basic T-shirt, formal shirt, jeans, kurta, nightwear, school wear, saree, innerwear) ka tag price note karo aur woh price bhi jahan tak bargain mein aate hain. Notebook mein likho. Phir apni landed cost se compare karo.

  • Agar unka price aapki cost se neeche hai, toh copy mat karo. Woh item mat rakho, ya sirf logon ko laane ke liye rakho.
  • Agar unke price par aapko achha margin bachta hai, toh match kar sakte ho aur service par compete karo.
  • Agar kisi item ka aas-paas koi achha version nahi hai, toh aapne ek gap dhoond liya.

Where a smaller shop can win

  • Range and sizes. Chhota shop har size nahi rakh sakta. Aap rakh sakte ho. Jinhe kahin aur apni size nahi milti woh aapka price denge.
  • Specific customers. Ek ya do groups ko achhe se serve karo: school aur college students, young men's casual wear, women's kurtis aur ethnic wear, festival aur wedding wear. Kuch cheezein achhi karna sab kuch average karne se behtar hai.
  • Quick service. Alterations, aasaan exchange, friendly trial room, saaf shop. Inme kam kharch lagta hai aur customers inki baat karte hain.
  • Trust. Agar aapki quality steady hai aur prices fair, toh regular customers har shirt compare karna band kar dete hain.
  • Value you can explain. Agar aapka cloth behtar hai, toh batao kyun: stitching, fabric, wash. Wajah batane se aap higher price rakh paate ho.
Avoid a price war

M Baazar bade quantity mein kharid sakta hai aur kam margin accept kar sakta hai. Price war dono shops ko garib karti hai aur bada shop zyada der tikta hai. Calculator 4 se apna break-even nikalo, phir wahan compete karo jahan aap phir bhi uske upar reh sakte ho.

Fixed price or bargaining?

Bargaining un customers ko jeet leti hai jinhe game pasand hai, aur un se zyada kamane deti hai jo aasani se pay kar dete hain. Saaf chhapa fixed price time bachata hai, trust banata hai, aur busy buyers ke liye achha hai. Aap mix kar sakte ho: kuch items par bargain, aur basics par "fixed price". Jo bhi choose karo, price chapter 6 ke method se set karo.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

M Baazar ne sale laga di. Kya main bhi lagaun?

Nahi, automatically nahi. Pehle dekho woh kin items par sale hai. Un items par aapka floor kya hai? Agar unka sale price aapke floor se neeche hai, toh aap match nahi kar sakte. Doosre items par normal price rakho, aur apne regular customers ko apni service ke baare mein yaad dilao. Chhoti shop sirf price se bade shop se nahi jeet sakti.

Online (Instagram/WhatsApp) se bechna chahiye kya?

Shuru mein sirf customers ke WhatsApp number lo (permission ke saath) aur festival se pehle naye stock ki photos bhejo. Free hai aur repeat customers laata hai. Online selling ke liye packing, delivery aur returns ki alag planning chahiye, isliye pehle shop sambhal jaye, phir sochna.

Doubt? Yeh poochhna normal hai

Excel mein sab kar sakta hoon, paise kyun kharchun?

Excel mein bill banana, har bill ke baad stock kam karna, invoice number maintain karna aur kisi galti se kuch overwrite na hone dena, sab aapko khud karna padega, aur counter par customer ke saamne. Kuch hi hafte mein yeh dikkat dene lagta hai. Billing app yeh sab apne aap kar deta hai. Excel analysis ke liye bahut achha hai.

Agar phone ya laptop kharab ho gaya toh data chala jayega?

Isliye aisa app lo jisme cloud backup ho (data internet par bhi save hota hai), ya haftey mein ek baar backup download karo. Aur ek simple notebook mein roz ka total sales aur cash likhna mat chhodo. Yeh aapka dusra backup hai.

Staff ko app sikhana mushkil nahi hoga?

Zyaadatar billing apps simple hote hain. Pehle 2 se 3 din aap khud billing karo, phir staff ko saath baitha ke sikhao. Pehle sirf "bill banao" sikhao, baaki features baad mein.

Practice problems

Har ek ko pehle paper par karo. Phir answer kholo. Is guide ke formulas use karo.

1. A shirt has landed cost ₹360 and sells for ₹480. Find profit, margin and markup.

Profit = 480 − 360 = ₹120.

Margin = 120 ÷ 480 × 100 = 25%.

Markup = 120 ÷ 360 × 100 = 33.3%.

2. Landed cost is ₹350. You want a 30% margin. What selling price?

SP = 350 ÷ (1 − 0.30) = 350 ÷ 0.70 = ₹500. Check: profit ₹150, aur 150 ÷ 500 = 30%.

3. Tag ₹799. The customer pays ₹650. What discount percent did they get?

Discount = 799 − 650 = ₹149. 149 ÷ 799 × 100 = 18.6%.

4. Landed cost ₹400. What is the lowest price that still gives 15% margin?

Floor = 400 ÷ 0.85 = 470.6, toh ₹471. Isse kam par 15% se kam margin milega.

5. Jeans: landed cost ₹580, tag ₹1,299. A customer offers ₹900. What are your profit and margin? Would you accept if your minimum margin is 25%?

Profit = 900 − 580 = ₹320. Margin = 320 ÷ 900 × 100 = 35.6%. Haan, accept karo. Yeh 25% se upar hai.

6. Landed cost ₹250. Minimum margin at floor 20%, biggest bargain 15%. What tag price?

Floor = 250 ÷ 0.80 = ₹312.5. Tag = 312.5 ÷ 0.85 = 367.6. Round up karo ₹369 ya ₹379 tak.

7. You give 10% off, then an extra 5% off, on ₹1,000. What does the customer pay?

10% ke baad: ₹900. 900 ka 5% = ₹45. Customer dega ₹855, yaani 14.5% off, 15% nahi.

8. Margin is 25%. You give a 10% discount. How many times your normal sales do you need to earn the same profit?

m ÷ (m − d) = 0.25 ÷ 0.15 = 1.67 guna. Aapko lagbhag do-tihai zyada bechna padega.

9. Fixed costs are ₹30,000 a month. Average margin is 28%. What monthly sales to break even, and per day over 26 days?

30,000 ÷ 0.28 = ₹1,07,143 a month. ÷ 26 = ₹4,121 a day.

10. You buy 50 kurtis at ₹400 each. Transport and packing come to ₹1,500. You expect 3 to stay unsold. What is the landed cost per sellable kurti?

Total = 50 × 400 + 1,500 = ₹21,500. Sellable = 47. 21,500 ÷ 47 = ₹457.

11. A supplier says he adds 25% markup on his cost. What is his margin?

Margin = markup ÷ (1 + markup) = 0.25 ÷ 1.25 = 20%. Check: cost ₹100, price ₹125, profit ₹25, aur 25 ÷ 125 = 20%.

12. Fixed costs are ₹40,000, expected sales ₹2,00,000. What is the survival margin? And the target margin if you also want ₹20,000 profit?

Survival = 40,000 ÷ 2,00,000 = 20%. Target = (40,000 + 20,000) ÷ 2,00,000 = 30%.

Seven habits of a shop that survives
  1. Price landed cost se set karo, kabhi sirf bill price se nahi.
  2. Customer aane se pehle apna floor price tay karo.
  3. Hamesha poochho "percent of what?"
  4. Kisi bhi discount se pehle profit check karo.
  5. Apna daily break-even number jaano.
  6. Stock ko baithne mat do. Schedule se markdown karo.
  7. Sab kuch likho: purchases, sales, udhar.

  • Sales ko break-even se compare karo.
  • Markdown deadline paar karne wale items clear karo.
