CONCEPT 1

Managing Extreme Uncertainty

Traditional corporate management is about executing a known business model (like managing an established hospital or bank branch). Entrepreneurial management is about searching for a repeatable, scalable business model in conditions of extreme uncertainty.

💡 ELI5 Breakdown: Traditional managers manage what already works. Entrepreneurs run experiments to figure out what might work!
CONCEPT 2

Idea Generation & Customer Validation

Great startups don't start with solutions; they start with unmet customer pain points. Before writing code for her Tiffin Delivery App, Priya spent 2 weeks interviewing 50 office workers in Mumbai to confirm if getting healthy home-cooked lunch was a real daily struggle.

CONCEPT 3

Market Sizing: TAM, SAM & SOM

Before launching a startup, investors will demand to know: Is the market big enough to care about?

TAM (Total Addressable Market)

If EVERY single potential customer in the world bought your product. (e.g. All 1.4 Billion people in India who eat lunch).

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

The portion of TAM you can reach with your tech & geography. (e.g. Office workers in Mumbai ordering lunch online).

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

Your realistic Year 1 target. (e.g. 5,000 tech workers in Bandra-Kurla Complex).

CONCEPT 4

The Lean Startup Cycle: Build → Measure → Learn

Eric Ries's Lean Startup framework advocates short feedback loops. Instead of spending 1 year secretly building a complex app, build the smallest thing, put it in front of users, measure behavior, learn, and iterate!

CONCEPT 5

Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

An MVP is the version of a new product which allows a team to collect the maximum amount of validated learning about customers with the least effort.

  • Landing Page MVP: Test demand with a simple sign-up form before building features.
  • Concierge MVP: Deliver the service manually behind the scenes to learn customer needs intimately.
  • Wizard of Oz MVP: Make the front-end look automated while humans perform the work in the back.
CONCEPT 6

Pivot vs. Persevere

When measurements show your hypothesis was wrong, do not give up on the vision — pivot! A pivot is a structured course correction designed to test a new fundamental hypothesis about the product or business model.

CONCEPT 7

Unit Economics: CAC vs LTV

The Golden Rule of Startups: LTV must be at least 3x CAC!

  • CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): Marketing spend divided by total new customers acquired.
  • LTV (Lifetime Value): Total cumulative profit a single customer brings before churning.

CONCEPT 8

Pitching & Startup Funding Stages

Startups progress through funding rounds as they de-risk the venture:

  • Bootstrapping / Pre-Seed: Founder's own savings + friends & family.
  • Seed Round: Angel investors & micro-VCs funding MVP & initial validation.
  • Series A: Institutional VCs funding scaling and aggressive growth after finding product-market fit.

⚡ Founder's War-Room Challenge

If your startup spends ₹1,000 on Google Ads to get 1 user, but that user only spends ₹400 before deleting the app... what should you do?

📌 War-Room Summary

  • Validate First: Talk to customers before writing code.
  • TAM/SAM/SOM: Make sure your target market is big enough.
  • Lean Cycle: Build → Measure → Learn.
  • LTV > 3x CAC: Never scale marketing until unit economics work.
