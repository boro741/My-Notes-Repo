💡 Beginner's Business Jargon Buster

Asset
Anything valuable the business owns (e.g. cash, tapri cart, inventory of tea leaves).
Liability
Money the business owes to outsiders (e.g. bank loan, unpaid milk bills).
Equity
What is left for the owner after paying all liabilities. (Assets - Liabilities = Equity).
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Total money spent on marketing divided by the number of new customers acquired.
LTV (Lifetime Value)
Total profit a single customer generates over their entire relationship with your business.
Break-Even Point
The exact sales volume where Total Revenue = Total Costs (Zero profit, zero loss).
NPV (Net Present Value)
The value today of future cash inflows minus current investment costs, considering inflation & interest.
WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital)
The average interest rate a business pays to raise funds from loans and investors.
