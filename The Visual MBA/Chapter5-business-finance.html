CONCEPT 1

1. The Time Value of Money (TVM)

A rupee today is worth MORE than a rupee tomorrow because of Inflation and Opportunity Cost (Interest). If you put ₹100 in a bank fixed deposit earning 10% interest, it becomes ₹110 next year. Therefore, receiving ₹100 next year is really worth only ~₹90.90 today!

CONCEPT 2

2. Net Present Value (NPV) & Investment Appraisal

NPV measures whether a future project will make your company richer or poorer in today's money:

NPV = (Present Value of Future Cash Inflows) − Initial Investment Cost Today

CONCEPT 3

3. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

IRR is the exact discount rate that makes a project's Net Present Value equal to zero (NPV = 0). If the IRR of a project is higher than your cost of borrowing money, accept the project!

CONCEPT 4

4. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

WACC is the overall average rate of return a business must pay to all its funding sources (debt lenders + equity investors).

CONCEPT 5

5. Capital Structure & Financial Leverage

Capital Structure is how a company finances its overall operations and growth using a mix of Debt (loans) and Equity (selling shares). Debt is cheaper because interest is tax-deductible, but too much debt increases bankruptcy risk!

CONCEPT 6

6. Working Capital Management & Cash Cycle

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities. It measures the short-term operational liquidity needed to keep suppliers paid while waiting for customers to pay.

CONCEPT 7

7. Business Valuation Methods

How much is a business worth? Three main methods are used:

  • DCF (Discounted Cash Flow): Valuing a business based on the present value of its future cash flows.
  • Comps / Multiples: Valuing a business relative to similar public companies (e.g. 10x PE ratio).
  • Asset-Based Valuation: Net value of total physical assets minus liabilities.
CONCEPT 8

8. Financial Risk & Hedging

Businesses use financial instruments (futures, options, swaps) to hedge against unpredictable fluctuations in interest rates, commodity prices, and foreign exchange currencies.

📌 Finance Summary

  • Time Value of Money: ₹1 today > ₹1 tomorrow.
  • NPV Rule: Accept projects where NPV > 0.
  • WACC: The cost of raising capital from lenders & equity.
  • Valuation: DCF discounts future free cash flows to today's present value.
