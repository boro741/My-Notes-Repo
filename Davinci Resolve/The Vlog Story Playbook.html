The Vlog Story Playbook

How to pick a video topic you won’t regret and turn it into a story people watch till the end. Written for entertainment vlogs, with tools you can play with. No script-writing skills needed.

1. The big idea: a story is not a list

Think of two friends telling you about their weekend. One says “we went here, then here, then ate this.” The other says “you won’t believe what happened at the railway station.” You remember the second one. Same weekend, different storytelling. A vlog is just that, with a camera. You don’t need a dramatic life. You need to pick the right moment and tell it in the right order.

2. Picking your topic

Most new vloggers lose weeks here. Use three tools: generate ideas, score them, then decide. Don’t trust the feeling of “this sounds good” alone.

3. The story core: want, obstacle, change

Every watchable video has three things: someone wants something, something gets in the way, and by the end something has changed. If your idea has no obstacle (“I went to a mall”), it’s a diary, not a story. Add a rule or limit and it becomes one.
