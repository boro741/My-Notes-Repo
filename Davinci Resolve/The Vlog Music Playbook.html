⚡ In a hurry? Remember just these 5 things

  1. Your voice is the star. Music stays quiet while you talk.
  2. Match the feeling of each scene. Happy scene, happy music. Sad scene, slow soft music.
  3. Change the music between parts of the video. One track from start to end gets boring.
  4. No singing ({{vocals|lyrics}}) under your talking. Choose {{instrumental}} tracks.
  5. Use only safe music so your video is not muted or claimed.

⏱️ Speed: what is BPM?

{{BPM}} means beats per minute: how many “dum” sounds happen in one minute. A bigger number means faster music. Your resting heartbeat is about 70 BPM. Drag the dial and listen to the click. Or tap the Tap button along with any song to measure its speed!

🎚️ Mixing: how loud should the music be?

Most new vloggers make the same mistake: the music is too loud and the voice gets buried. “Mixing” just means balancing the volume of voice and music. Press the button to hear a voice with music, then move the slider. {{dB|dB (decibel)}} is the unit for loudness; here, a more negative number means quieter.

The voice is your device’s built-in robot voice. This is a rough demo, so always test in your real editor too.
Voice (fixed)-9 dB
Music level-24 dB
  • Voice first. If a viewer has to strain to hear you, the music is too loud.
  • {{Duck|Duck the music}}. Lower it while you speak and raise it in pauses and {{b-roll}}. Most editors have an auto-ducking option.
  • {{Fade|Fade}}, don’t chop. End music with a smooth fade unless a sudden stop is deliberate.
  • Final loudness. YouTube plays videos near -14 {{LUFS}}, so there’s no need to push everything to maximum.
