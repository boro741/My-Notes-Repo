The Vlog Music Playbook

A beginner’s guide to picking music, mood and sound effects for your YouTube vlogs. No music knowledge needed. If you’ve ever stared at a music library thinking “everything sounds fine and nothing sounds right,” this is for you.

1. Why this feels so hard

Think of music like tadka on dal. The right tadka lifts everything; too much and you can’t taste the dal. Your video is the dal, and your voice and visuals are the main flavour. Music is there to make them taste better. The reason you feel stuck is simple: you haven’t built a “sound dictionary” yet, and that’s completely normal at the start.

6. Mixing: volume levels (play with it)

Most new vloggers make the same mistake: the music is too loud and the voice gets buried. Move the slider and watch where your music lands. These are starting points; always check on earphones and a phone speaker.

Voice (fixed)-9 dB
Music level-24 dB
  • Voice first. If a viewer has to strain to hear you, the music is too loud.
  • Duck the music. Lower it while you speak and raise it in pauses and b-roll. Most editors have an auto-ducking option.
  • Fade, don’t chop. End music with a smooth fade unless a sudden stop is deliberate.
  • Final loudness. YouTube plays videos near -14 LUFS, so there’s no need to push everything to maximum.
